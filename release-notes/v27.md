DQIX Enhanced v27: the second stable version. Everything below is new since
v26, after 21 nightly test versions.

**What's new**
- **Easy setup**: double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat` and a window
  walks you through it: choose your ROM (it says which version it is),
  where to put the game (Documents by default; it warns about OneDrive and
  folders Windows protects), shortcuts on the desktop and in the Start menu,
  then it builds the game with a progress bar and a Play now button. The
  game is listed in Settings > Apps, where it can be uninstalled (your saves
  are kept unless you say otherwise).
- **More routers let friends in**: when UPnP is switched off or missing, the
  game also asks the router with NAT-PMP and PCP. Friends with IPv6 can join
  over IPv6 too (the game asks the router's firewall to let them in), and
  joining accepts an IPv6 address.
- **Send a bug report** (Help menu, or F10): saves the report, then opens a
  ready-made report on GitHub with the zip to drag into it. It says first
  exactly what's included, since the report is public; your save is only
  included if you tick the box.
- **Any version of the ROM can be tried**: no more checksum check. The USA
  version is the tested one; others get a warning and are built anyway.
- **Clearer windows**: the update window's buttons fit their text
  ("Update to v27", "Skip this version", "Later"), and when a new stable
  version is offered, a button to install the newest nightly instead.
- **Playing with friends far away**: tag mode (canvassing for guests) and
  co-op through Pavo with up to 4 players over the internet. The game sets up
  your router where it can, and the host gets a window with the address to
  send to friends and a Start button.
- **DQVC like the original**: sales on the original calendar (Fridays,
  08:00 UTC), new stock every day, every player their own shop, visitors can
  shop at the host's Sellma, and DQVC works inside multiplayer sessions.
- **Modern options** (new Modern menu):
  - **Widescreen**: the world is drawn wider than the DS's screen, so you see
    more of it left and right, and the top screen sits small in the corner
    like a minimap. Menus slide over to make room; in battles the commands
    sit in the corner at half size. 16:9 in a window, as wide as your
    monitor in fullscreen. Enhanced mode.
  - **Walk in any direction** with a controller's left stick, now on at
    first and without the stylus's arrow on the screen.
  - The top screen in the corner, with the field as on the DS.
- **Nothing extra to download**: DQVC and the downloadable quests are built
  into the game; the build fetches DQVC's news by itself.
- **Updates from inside the game**: Help > Check for updates (stable or
  nightly), and a notice when a new stable version is out.
- **The game's own icon**: dqix.exe gets the icon the DS menu shows for the
  cartridge, made from your ROM when the game is built. Double-click
  `game\dqix.exe` (or a shortcut to it) to play: no console window.
- **New installs start with the DS Lite look**: the DS Lite screen filter
  and the screens inside the photo of a real DS Lite (F6 and F7 switch them).
  Updating doesn't change your settings.
- **New installs start with Wireless > Connected mode on**, so Sellma's
  downloads and DQVC work straight away (switch it off in the Wireless menu).
- The photo of a real DS Lite is the default console; tidier help windows.
- **The right stick turns the camera** while you walk around (like L and R,
  slower with a light push). In fullscreen, **double-click** outside the
  touch screen to go back to the window.
- Fixed: a rare freeze after "Continue your adventure" (it took an
  interrupt arriving at one exact moment, e.g. after skipping the logos with
  a tap on one particular frame).
- Fixed: the sticks could tap and press L/R on the title screen; they only
  walk and turn the camera once an adventure is loaded.
- Fixed: A and B (and X and Y) were swapped on Switch controllers (Pro
  Controller, Joy-Cons). Every controller now uses the DS layout by
  position: right button A, bottom button B.
- Fixed: a thin line of one colour along the top of a screen when the
  screens are shown smaller than they are drawn (with the console photo).

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`. A window walks you through it.
**Already on v26?** Download v27 the same way (v26 can't update itself), and
copy `dqix.sav` and `settings.ini` from your old `game` folder into the new
one. From v27 on, updates come from inside the game: Help > Check for updates.

**Still being checked**: internet play has only been tested on one PC so far,
and router set-up (UPnP, NAT-PMP, PCP, IPv6) against real routers by few
people. If something goes wrong, Help > Send a bug report.

The plans for what comes next are in ROADMAP.md.
