Dragon Quest IX for PC
======================

This turns YOUR OWN Dragon Quest IX ROM (dumped from your cartridge; the
USA or European version) into a PC version of the game. No game files are included.

1. Double-click "Install Dragon Quest IX.bat". A window walks you through it:
   your ROM, where to put the game (Documents by default) and shortcuts.
   - The first time it installs free build tools (MSYS2) and takes 10-20 minutes.
   - It needs Windows 10/11 (64-bit), ~4 GB free space and the internet once.
2. Press "Play now" at the end. Later, use the desktop or Start menu shortcut.
3. To update to a newer version later: unzip it anywhere and double-click
   "Update Dragon Quest IX.bat". It finds your game, keeps your saves and ROM.

Without the window: put your ROM (.nds, or a .zip with it inside) in the "rom"
folder, double-click BUILD.bat, then play game\dqix.exe.

MSYS2 already installed somewhere unusual? Put its folder in msys2-path.txt
next to BUILD.bat (one line, e.g. D:\tools\msys64).

Controls: arrows = D-pad, X = A, Z = B, S = X, A = Y, Q/W = L/R,
Enter = START, Right Shift = SELECT, mouse = touch screen, Tab = fast forward (on/off).
Gamepads work too. Every setting is in the menu bar at the top of the window.

If something goes wrong, press F10 in the game: it saves a bug report
(with a box to describe what happened) and can open GitHub for you with it
copied: press Ctrl+V in the page, then Create.

Please don't share the "game" or "build" folders: they contain the game.
