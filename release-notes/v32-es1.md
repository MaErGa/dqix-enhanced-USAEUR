# DQIX Enhanced (USA and EUR) v32-es1

Dragon Quest IX as a native PC game, built on your PC from your own ROM (USA or European version).
No game files are included. Install with `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`; to update an installed game,
unzip the new version anywhere and double-click `Update Dragon Quest IX.bat`.

## What this version has

- **USA and European versions.** For the European one, *Game > Language* chooses English, French,
  German, Italian or Spanish (the first run follows your system's language).
- **Interface in English and Spanish** (menu *Interface language / Idioma*; it follows Windows the first
  time). The installer and uninstaller are in Spanish when Windows is. Adding a language: docs/TRANSLATING.md.
- **Update Dragon Quest IX.bat**: finds the installed game (or asks where it is), copies the new files over
  it, builds again, and keeps your saves, settings and ROM. In the game, *Help > Check for updates* and the
  bug reports use this repository.
- **Fast forward speed** (*Game > Speed of the fast forward*: 2x, 4x or unlimited); Tab turns the fast
  forward on and off.
- **Lighter online sessions**: the console of the other players is never looked at, so it is not drawn.
  The same stretch of game costs about a third of the CPU.
- Save export / import (also DeSmuME and melonDS saves), HD text, soft shadows, character outlines,
  widescreen (basic and dynamic), battle HUD in widescreen, texture packs, upscaled 3D textures and music
  packs (menu *Mods*), Share button, and the controls dialog (*Game > Controls*).

## Known limits

- Tested without a window (USA and European: boot, load a save, play) and compiled for Windows; the
  installer, updater and online sessions between two PCs were not tried on a real Windows PC.
- The Japanese version is not supported.
- There are no instant save states: the game's threads can't be saved safely. Export your save before
  risking something (*Game > Save file*).

## En español

Dragon Quest IX como juego nativo de PC, compilado en tu PC con tu propia ROM (USA o europea). Interfaz en
español, instalador y desinstalador en español, `Update Dragon Quest IX.bat` para actualizar sin volver a
instalar, velocidad del avance rápido (2x, 4x, sin límite) y sesiones online más ligeras. Con la ROM
europea, *Juego > Idioma* elige el idioma del propio juego. No hay guardado instantáneo ni versión japonesa.
